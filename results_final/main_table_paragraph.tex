Table~\ref{tab:main} reports the main results.
For the Qwen3-1.7B student, \ours{} raises the average of OPD from $50.08$ to
$53.55$.
The gains are largest on the competition benchmarks, with $+5.04$ on AIME 2024,
$+5.41$ on AIME 2025, and $+4.86$ on AMC 2023, while MATH500 improves by $2.22$
and Minerva Math by $0.61$.
Among the students, \ours{} obtains the best accuracy on
three of the six benchmarks.
Its average is $1.00$ points above that of FastOPD and $0.13$ points below that of RelayOPD, which is better on AIME 2025 and Minerva Math.
Both baselines change how rollouts are generated, whereas \ours{} changes how
incorrect rollouts are supervised.
