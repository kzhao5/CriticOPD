Table~\ref{tab:main} reports the main results.
For the Qwen3-1.7B student, \ours{} raises the average of OPD from $50.08$ to
$54.65$.
The gains are largest on the competition benchmarks, with $+6.01$ on AIME 2024,
$+7.36$ on AIME 2025, and $+6.94$ on AMC 2023, while MATH500 improves by $2.61$
and Minerva Math by $0.66$.
Among the students, \ours{} obtains the best average and the best accuracy on
five of the six benchmarks.
Its average is $2.10$ points above that of FastOPD and $0.97$ points above that of RelayOPD, which is better only on Minerva Math.
For the Qwen3-0.6B student, \ours{} raises the average of OPD from $34.80$ to $38.46$, which is $0.20$ points above that of RelayOPD and $1.17$ points above that of FastOPD.
Both baselines change how rollouts are generated, whereas \ours{} changes how
incorrect rollouts are supervised.
