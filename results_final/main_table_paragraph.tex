Table~\ref{tab:main} reports the main results.
For the Qwen3-1.7B student, \ours{} raises the average of OPD from $50.08$ to
$53.72$.
The gains are largest on the competition benchmarks, with $+6.46$ on AIME 2024,
$+4.82$ on AIME 2025, and $+4.49$ on AMC 2023, while MATH500 improves by $2.49$
and Minerva Math by $1.13$.
Among the students, \ours{} obtains the best average and the best accuracy on
four of the six benchmarks.
Its average is $1.17$ points above that of FastOPD and $0.04$ points above that of RelayOPD, which is better on AIME 2025 and OlympiadBench.
Both baselines change how rollouts are generated, whereas \ours{} changes how
incorrect rollouts are supervised.
