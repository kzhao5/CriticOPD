  教训示例:
    [algebra or sign error] When squaring and combining terms with fractions, always compute each squared term individually and add them before simplifying, rather than combining the denominators incorrectly.
    [wrong formula or theorem] Before concluding that a bound is achievable, verify that the proposed value satisfies a necessary constraint derived from the total sum and the structure of the subsets.
    [unjustified assumption] Before claiming a problem reduces to a certain mathematical setup, verify that the proposed equivalence is both logically valid and complete, especially when the original question involves counting elements with specific properties.
    [domain or boundary error] When dealing with sequential selections, the sample space must account for the order of draws and the dependencies between steps, not just the final outcome or a single combination.
    [algebra or sign error] Always verify that the entries in the matrix correspond correctly to the positions used in the determinant expansion, especially when applying the cofactor formula.
    [algebra or sign error] When using trigonometry to place a point based on an angle, always consider the quadrant and orientation to ensure the correct sign of the coordinate.
    [arithmetic slip] Always double-check arithmetic when evaluating an expression at a given value, especially when the result is expected to be a prime number.
    [domain or boundary error] Always verify that all values in a sequence or set satisfy the given constraints before including them in a count.
